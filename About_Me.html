My Story

I began my journey in graphic design, working across branding and social media, but I quickly realized something was missing — storytelling. It wasn’t until I started bringing illustration into my work that I understood what made my designs stand out. Today, I create visuals that go beyond aesthetics, blending illustration, lettering, and culture to craft meaningful brand narratives. I’m especially drawn to projects that explore identity, tradition, and storytelling in modern ways. Outside of work, I’m a manga reader, researcher, and a slightly moody traveller.

I turn cultural stories
into packaging people can hold.
-Shweta
